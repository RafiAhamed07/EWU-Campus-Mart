About Us

EWU Campus Mart is a secure, university-based eCommerce platform where students can buy and sell products within their campus community. It allows student entrepreneurs to showcase their businesses while others can easily browse and purchase items.

Access is limited to verified students using university credentials, ensuring a safe and trusted marketplace. The platform promotes convenience, peer-to-peer trading, and real-world business experience for students.
